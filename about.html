About the studio

A meaningful niche in every home we shape

Arthanisa is an architecture and interior design studio based in Thane, Mumbai — founded on the belief that good design starts with meaning, not just measurements.

The word itself is a promise: Arth — meaningful, Nisa — home.

ARTHANISA, the word, has its roots in the Sanskrit language — an amalgamation of two words: Arth, which means meaningful, and Nisa, which translates to home. We believe in creating meaningful, niche spaces in people’s lives — that’s their homes. That’s what Arthanisa literally translates into.

ARTHANISA is the brainchild of Ar. Meher Choupane, co-founded by Ar. Shirin Shaikh, based in Thane, Mumbai — with a view to provide quality architectural services under one roof, such as planning, designing, detailing and liaison services. Our approach prioritises simplicity, warmth and honesty. We, as a firm, provide architectural and interior design solutions regardless of the scale and size of the project.

Ar. Meher Choupane and Ar. Shirin Shaikh are the Principal Architects and founding partners of this firm. At ARTHANISA, we believe in providing top-quality service in a fair and responsible way, without compromising on our values and principles.
